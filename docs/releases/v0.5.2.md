# v0.5.2: a target reference rest, and the T-pose directions as vocabulary

**Release status:** prepared on 2026-09-30. Not yet tagged.

## Objective

Let a retarget target state a humanoid reference rest that differs from its
UsdSkel rest, and make the canonical T-pose directions public, so that a
format repository whose rigs rest in an A-pose can be both a source and a
target of an exact retarget without copying anything from here.

## Why it is needed now

`usd-mmd-plugins` is the first consumer that never held a copy of this code.
Every PMX rests at identity rotation with its arms 37–42° below horizontal; a
VRM or a `t-pose` BVH rests at identity with level arms. `RestPoseCorrection`
compared rest rotations only, so the two were one pose to it, and the upper
arms landed about 40° off in both directions
([issue #32](https://github.com/animu-sphere/usd-motion-plugins/issues/32)).

That repository measured it on 17 characters and two dance motions against
MMD's own evaluated segment directions
(`docs/reports/2026-09-25-phase9-rest-pose-comparison.md` there):

| Upper arm, median / max | Identity rests | Arm-chain rest stated on the source | Stated on source **and** target |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| MMD → level-arm rig | 40.16° / 42.42° | 0.00° / 0.04° | — |
| PMX → PMX | 2.45° / 5.26° | 40.16° / 42.42° | 0.00° / 0.04° |
| level-arm clip → PMX | 40.16° / 42.42° | — | 0.00° / 0.04° |

The source half existed at v0.5.1: `SourceRestPose` could state a rest aimed
onto the T-pose. Stated on the source alone it made PMX → PMX as wrong as the
other two cases, so a PMX target had to state the same rest — and a target's
only rest was `SkeletonJoint::restRotation`, which must equal what the stage's
`UsdSkelSkeleton` says. Writing the aim there in the caller made every reader
of the stage see a different descriptor, and posed an undriven joint at the
aim. The T-pose directions were private to `motionSource`, and the consumer
may not copy them. Its MOT-O10 waited on both.

## What ships

Every library, the bundle and the three CLIs, rebuilt at 0.5.2. Two public
additions and one move; no existing call changes its result.

- **`TargetRestPose`** (`motionRetarget/RestPose.h`): optional local
  rotations in the target skeleton's joint order, with `GetLocalRestRotation`
  and `GetWorldRestRotation` over a `SkeletonDescriptor`. An unset slot uses
  the UsdSkel rest. `RetargetOptions::targetRest` carries it into
  `PoseRetargeter`, and `ComputeRestPoseCorrection` gains an overload that
  takes it for `T` and `Tp`. The descriptor still describes the stage's rest,
  and a joint the clip does not drive still takes its rotation from the
  descriptor. An empty `TargetRestPose` reproduces the previous correction
  exactly
  ([RETARGETING_POLICY.md §5](https://github.com/animu-sphere/usd-motion-plugins/blob/v0.5.2/docs/design/RETARGETING_POLICY.md#5-rest-pose-correction)).
- **`motionSource::TPoseDirection` and `ShortestRotation`**
  (`motionSource/CanonicalConversion.h`): the canonical direction of a bone's
  outgoing segment — zero where the vocabulary specifies none — and the swing
  between two unit directions. A format adapter selects the bones it aims and
  owns the segment geometry and any roll. For MMD that is the arm chain alone:
  the same aim on the whole body flips the chest of a model whose `上半身3`
  lies below `上半身2`, and levels feet a humanoid rest leaves sloped.
- **`motionCore` owns signed-permutation basis conversion** (CS-O1).
  `SignedPermutationBasis` and its validated operations serve the recorded
  readers now; `motion-connectors` adopts them when its pins move to 0.5.2.
  `motionSource` keeps its profile interpretation and its public
  `CanonicalBasis`.

The rest of the changelog section is documentation and build plumbing: one
owner per subject, a build that writes nothing into the source tree, the
`ost` pin at 0.23.6, and `cmake/` shared across every member.

## How it was checked before the tag

- The whole workspace ran under `ost` 0.23.14 on Windows at 0.5.2: root
  `ost build` and `ost test`, 54 of 54.
- `motionRetarget_unit` gains `TestTargetReferenceRestIsSeparateFromUsdSkelRest`:
  a stated target rest corrects a driven joint by the reference rest, an
  ancestor's override reaches its descendants' world reference rotation, a
  source stating the same rest needs no offset, an undriven joint keeps the
  UsdSkel rest, and an empty `TargetRestPose` equals the previous correction.
  `motionSource_conversion` checks `TPoseDirection` for the upper arms and a
  finger, and `ShortestRotation` from a diagonal onto the lateral axis.
- `scripts/check_docs.py`: every link and anchor resolves; VERSION and every
  mirror agree at 0.5.2.

Nothing in the publish path changes from v0.5.1: the same three targets, the
same OCI repository and tag shape, the same pin table.

## What the consumer does next

`usd-mmd-plugins` moves its `motionCore`, `motionRetarget` and `motionUsd`
pins to 0.5.2, adds `motionSource` for the aim helpers, and has
`mmdSkeletonAdapter` state the arm-chain rest on both sides: as a
`SourceRestPose` for a PMX source, and as a `TargetRestPose` for a PMX target.
Its MOT-O10 closes with that.
